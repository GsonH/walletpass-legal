Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Last updated: 10 November 2026

中文版

Overview

walletPass is an iOS app that turns barcodes, photos and PDF tickets into passes you can add to Apple Wallet. The app is operated by Wuhan Qiance Network Technology Co., Ltd. ("we", "us").

The short version: what you scan or type stays on your device. We do not run an account system and we do not upload your cards, photos, documents or barcodes.

Information the app handles

DataWhere it goesWhy
Photos, PDF files and camera frames you choose or capture Stays on your device Barcode detection runs locally with Apple's Vision framework. Nothing is uploaded and no copy is kept after processing.
Pass details you enter (titles, fields, dates, barcode values, colors) Stays on your device Used to build and sign the pass, and to keep your library when you reopen the app.
Passes you add to Apple Wallet Stored by Apple Wallet Handled by Apple under Apple's terms. walletPass cannot read your other Wallet passes.
Purchase and subscription information Sent to RevenueCat, Inc. Used to validate purchases, restore purchases and unlock Pro features.

Subscriptions and RevenueCat

walletPass Pro is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and entitlements. When you open the app or make a purchase, RevenueCat receives:

  • An anonymous app user ID generated by the app
  • Your purchase history for this app (products, prices, renewal status)
  • Limited device and app information such as device model, operating system version, app version and country
  • Technical request data, which may include your IP address

RevenueCat does not receive your pass contents, photos or documents. Payment details are handled by Apple; we never see your card number. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address or phone number
  • No contacts, calendars, location or health data
  • No advertising identifiers and no third-party advertising SDKs
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No upload of your photos, PDF files or pass contents to our servers

Retention and deletion

Passes and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them. Subscription records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their policies; you can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your Apple ID settings.

Your choices

  • Revoke camera or photo access at any time in iOS Settings → walletPass.
  • Delete individual passes in the app, or delete the app to remove all local data.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Contact us to ask what purchase-related data is associated with your anonymous app user ID.

Children

walletPass is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, describe them in the app's release notes.

English

概述

walletPass 是一款 iOS 应用，把条码、照片和 PDF 电子票做成可以加入 Apple 钱包的卡券。本应用由武汉黔策网络科技有限公司运营。

简单说：你扫描或填写的内容只留在你的设备上。我们没有账号系统，也不会上传你的卡券、照片、文件或条码。

应用处理哪些信息

数据去向用途
你选择或拍摄的照片、PDF 文件与相机画面 仅本机 用 Apple Vision 在本机识别条码，不上传，处理完不保留副本。
你填写的卡券内容（标题、字段、日期、条码值、颜色） 仅本机 用于生成并签名卡券，以及下次打开应用时恢复卡券库。
你加入 Apple 钱包的卡券 由 Apple 钱包保存 按 Apple 的条款处理。walletPass 无法读取你钱包里的其他卡券。
购买与订阅信息 发送给 RevenueCat, Inc. 用于校验购买、恢复购买和解锁 Pro 功能。

订阅与 RevenueCat

walletPass Pro 通过 Apple 应用内购买销售。我们使用 RevenueCat 管理订阅和权益。打开应用或购买时，RevenueCat 会收到：

  • 应用生成的匿名用户标识
  • 本应用的购买记录（产品、价格、续订状态）
  • 有限的设备与应用信息，如设备型号、系统版本、应用版本和国家/地区
  • 请求所需的技术数据，可能包含 IP 地址

RevenueCat 不会收到你的卡券内容、照片或文件。支付信息由 Apple 处理，我们看不到你的银行卡号。详见 RevenueCat 隐私政策与 Apple 隐私政策。

我们不收集什么

  • 不收集账号、姓名、邮箱或电话
  • 不收集通讯录、日历、位置或健康数据
  • 不收集广告标识符，也不接入第三方广告 SDK
  • 不接入统计分析或崩溃上报 SDK
  • 不把照片、PDF 或卡券内容上传到我们的服务器

保留与删除

卡券和设置只保存在你的设备上，删除应用即删除。订阅记录由 Apple 与 RevenueCat 按其政策保留；你可以随时在 Apple ID 设置里管理或取消订阅。

你的选择

  • 随时在 iOS 设置 → walletPass 中关闭相机或照片权限。
  • 在应用内删除单张卡券，或删除应用以清除全部本地数据。
  • 在 iOS 设置 → Apple ID → 订阅 中管理或取消订阅。
  • 联系我们，查询与你匿名用户标识关联的购买数据。

儿童

walletPass 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

政策变更

如本政策发生变化，我们会更新上方的生效日期；涉及重大变更时，会在应用更新说明中写明。
